Who won public cleaning contracts in the Nordics, November 2026

Nordic public buyers published 17 contract award notices for building and window cleaning on TED between 29 October and 25 November 2026. Each is listed here with the winning suppliers, the value the buyer reported and the number of bids.

Six of the 17 notices come from Sweden, five from Finland, three from Denmark and three from Norway. No buyer in Iceland published a cleaning award in the period.

The largest awards

In Denmark, Roskilde Universitet awarded its cleaning contract to ProfilService A/S at 50.5M DKK after five bids. In Sweden, Region Norrbotten awarded cleaning in Luleå to Samhall Aktiebolag and Coor Service Management AB, with a notice value of 48.5M SEK. In Finland, the City of Espoo awarded cleaning for the Leppävaara and Tapiola areas to ISS Palvelut Oy, SOL Palvelut Oy and Alltime Suomi Oy at 17.3M EUR. All three Norwegian notices come from Domstoladministrasjonen, the courts administration. The largest of them, for Romerike and Glåmdal District Court, is 9.5M NOK.

Suppliers named more than once

4Service Eir Renhold AS is a winner in all three Norwegian notices. Each is for a district court and runs 72 months. In Finland, three suppliers appear twice: Siskon Siivous Oy (Helsinki's Culture and Leisure Division, and HSY), ISS Palvelut Oy (the Police University College and Espoo) and SOL Palvelut Oy (Helsinki and Espoo).

Bids and contract terms

Every notice states how many bids came in. The highest count is 14, for the cleaning of Oodi Central Library in Helsinki, which N-Clean Oy won at 1.45M EUR. Helsinki's Culture and Leisure Division received 11 bids for its cleaning services contract. The lowest count is three, for two of the Norwegian court contracts. Five of the 17 notices state a contract term.

What the list covers

Contract award notices classified under CPV group 9091 (cleaning services) that name a winner. We left out notices under the same code for other work: damage restoration, sewer flushing, chimney sweeping, floor refurbishment and cleaning products. TED covers contracts above the EU thresholds, so smaller contracts published only on national portals are not included.
