Know who won the contract last time, at what price, against how many bids

Before you price a public tender in the Nordics, Tendernord gives you the competitor file: the previous awards by the same buyer and in the same trade, with the winning supplier, the award value, the number of bids and the contract term. Every line comes from an official contract award notice and carries its TED number.

Your first competitor file is free

Send us the tender you are looking at (a TED notice number or a link is enough) or your trade and region. You get the file by email within one working day.

Request a free competitor file

Or write to alicesnack0225@gmail.com.

What is in a file

The buyer's earlier awards for the same kind of work, and awards by neighbouring buyers in your trade: who won, for how much, how many bids came in, and how long the contract runs where the notice states it.

When it helps

When you decide whether to bid and what price to enter. You see who you are up against and what the buyer has paid before, while there is still time to act on it.

Where it comes from

Contract award notices that public buyers must publish on TED. We do not estimate. If a notice leaves a figure out, the file says so.
