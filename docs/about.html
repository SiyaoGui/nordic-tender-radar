About

Nordic Tender Radar is a small, independent service in Copenhagen. We make public procurement data from the Nordic countries easy to search and easy to act on.

Public buyers in the EU must publish larger contracts on TED, the EU's official journal. The data is public, but it is spread over 24 languages and hard to filter, so small companies often miss contracts they could have won. This site lists every Nordic notice that is still open, and the tender finder on Apify turns the same data into daily alerts and exports.

Where the data comes from

Every listing comes from the official TED search service of the Publications Office of the European Union. We do not edit notices. We shorten titles, pick the English translation where TED has one, and add sector labels based on CPV codes.
