About

Tendernord is a public contract intelligence service based in Copenhagen. We work with suppliers who bid for public contracts in Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland and Iceland.

Public buyers must publish their larger contracts, and the outcome of each one, on TED, the EU's official journal. The award notices say who won, at what value and how many suppliers bid. That information is public, but it sits in thousands of separate notices in several languages. We bring it together around the one decision a supplier has to make: whether to bid, and at what price.

How we work

Every figure in a competitor file is taken from an official notice and shown with its TED notice number, so you can check it yourself. We do not estimate values or contract end dates. Where a notice leaves something out, we say so.

Where the data comes from

The official TED search service of the Publications Office of the European Union. The site is rebuilt from it every morning. We shorten titles, use the English version where TED has one, and add sector labels based on the CPV codes the buyer chose.
